# Hi, I'm Sumair Aziz 👋

I am a research professional working in **Embedded Systems, Biosignal Processing, and Artificial Intelligence (AI/ML/DL)** for healthcare and human-centred technologies. My work focuses on developing intelligent systems that analyse physiological signals to support objective assessment of human conditions.

Currently, I am pursuing a **PhD at the University of Canberra, Australia**, under the supervision of:

- [Dr Raul Fernandez Rojas](https://scholar.google.com.pk/citations?user=xfYTKhAAAAAJ&hl=en)  
- [Prof Girija Chetty](https://scholar.google.com.pk/citations?user=AkLmP_sAAAAJ&hl=en)  
- [Prof Roland Goecke](https://scholar.google.com.pk/citations?user=p-BUHOcAAAAJ&hl=en)

My research involves **biosignal analysis, multimodal sensing, machine learning, and embedded AI systems**, with applications in areas such as **pain assessment, healthcare monitoring, and intelligent diagnostic systems**.
